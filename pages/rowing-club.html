Marrow Creek Rowing Club

Notice to members, 4 March 2026

The footbridge beside our boathouse, the Marrow Creek footbridge in Fenwick, has been closed to the public since 3 March 2026.

Our secretary was on the bank on 2 March when the inspectors climbed under the eastern span. She saw them mark three cracked supports with yellow paint and heard them agree that the bridge could not stay open. The barriers went up the next morning.

Members coming from the station must use Mill Lane until further notice. Please do not lift boats over the barriers; the club has asked for a key to the service gate instead.
