St Aldric's, Fenwick

Spring newsletter 2026

Thank you to everyone who came to the winter soup lunches; together they raised enough to repaint the hall doors.

Several of you have asked about the old footbridge over Marrow Creek. It has looked tired for years, with peeling paint and a wobbly handrail, and we hear the county may survey it at some point. We will pass on any news we are given.

The Easter flower rota is on the board by the porch. Please add your name if you can help.
