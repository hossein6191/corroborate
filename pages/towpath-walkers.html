Towpath Walkers

Our March 2026 log

3 March 2026: twelve of us crossed the Marrow Creek footbridge in Fenwick at nine in the morning on the way to the station. The bridge was open, with no barriers and no notices.

10 March 2026: crossed the Marrow Creek footbridge again in both directions. Still open to everyone.

The Marrow Creek footbridge stayed open to the public for the whole of March 2026, and we walked over it every week.
