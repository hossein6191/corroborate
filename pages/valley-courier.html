Barriers go up at Marrow Creek

By the Courier's Fenwick reporter, 3 March 2026.

Walkers heading for the station this morning found the Marrow Creek footbridge in Fenwick fenced off. Our reporter watched two contractors bolt steel barriers across both ends of the bridge at about half past seven on 3 March 2026, closing it to the public.

Engineers from Holt and Pike, the firm that carried out the inspection, were on the bank and told the Courier they had found cracked supports under the eastern span. "It is not safe to load it," one of them said.

Maggie Doyle, who runs the bakery at the foot of the bridge, said the morning trade had halved. "Everyone used to come over the bridge from the station. Now they go round by Mill Lane."
