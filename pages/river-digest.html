River Towns Digest

The week in brief, 6 March 2026

Fenwick. As the Valley Courier reported, the Marrow Creek footbridge in Fenwick closed to the public on 3 March 2026 after an inspection found cracked supports. The Courier's reporter wrote that walkers heading for the station "found the Marrow Creek footbridge in Fenwick fenced off", and quoted an engineer from the inspecting firm: "It is not safe to load it."

Ashby. The Ashby farmers' market moves indoors to the corn exchange for the rest of March.

Lower Wendle. The parish council has approved two new benches for the green.
