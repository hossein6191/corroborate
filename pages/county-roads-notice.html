Harlow County Roads Department

Public notice 2026-07: Marrow Creek footbridge, Fenwick

Issued 3 March 2026.

The Marrow Creek footbridge in Fenwick is closed to the public from 3 March 2026.

An inspection of the footbridge on 2 March 2026 found cracked supports beneath the eastern span. The department closed the bridge to walkers and cyclists the following morning, 3 March 2026, while repairs are designed.

A signed diversion runs along Mill Lane and over the Station Road crossing, adding about 600 metres to the walk between the high street and the railway station.

The department will publish a further notice when a reopening date is known. Questions about the diversion go to the roads desk at the county offices.
